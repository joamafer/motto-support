Frequently asked questions

How does Motto work?
Every day brings a new Motto: a motivational quote with its own painted scene. Open the app and tap today's card to unlock it. Today's Motto is always free.
I missed a day. Can I still read it?
Yes. Days you missed stay in the Calendar. Use one feather to unlock any of them. Feathers also let you unlock a day ahead if you can't wait.
What are feathers and how do I get more?
Feathers are Motty's magic: each one unlocks one day. You get 3 as a welcome gift. After that, you can buy packs of 10, 25 or 40 in the Feather shop (Settings → Feather shop). They are one-off purchases, not a subscription, and they never expire.
What is Motty's tail?
Motty starts without a tail. Unlock every day of a month and that month's feather is added to his tail, each one painted with its own motif. Any year counts, in any order. With all twelve, Motty fans his full tail, you can share your "My Motty" postcard and use his full tail as the app icon. Tap Motty at the top of the Calendar to see your tail.
How do I set the daily reminder?
Go to Settings and turn on Daily reminder, then choose the time. If nothing arrives, check that notifications are allowed for Motto in your iPhone's Settings.
How do I save or share a Motto?
Tap the heart on a card to save it; you'll find it in Settings → Saved Mottos. Tap the share button to send it as a painted postcard.
How do I restore my feathers on a new device?
Your days and feathers are kept in your iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple ID and iCloud, open Motto and they come back on their own. If a purchase seems missing, tap Restore purchases in Settings.
Why can't Motto unlock a day right now?
To unlock, Motto checks today's date online, so you need a connection. If your device's date is set wrong, Motto will ask you to fix it. Days you already unlocked can be read offline.
I used the previous version of Motto. Is my progress still there?
Yes. The new Motto brings over the days you unlocked, your feathers and your reminder time from the previous version the first time it opens.
Is Motto available in Spanish?
Yes. Motto is in English and Spanish and follows your iPhone's language. To change it, go to Settings → Motto → Language on your iPhone.
Is my data collected?
No personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress lives on your device and in your own iCloud. The only exception is purchases, which are confirmed with RevenueCat using a random ID that isn't linked to you. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
