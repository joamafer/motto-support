Privacy Policy

Your daily Motto, and nothing else.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Motto has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What the app keeps lives on your device and in your own iCloud. The only other services it talks to are Apple and RevenueCat, which confirms feather purchases.

Overview

Motto brings you one motivational quote a day, with a painted scene and Motty the peacock. It is designed so that you never have to tell us anything about yourself.

Data we don't collect

Motto does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Usage analytics or behaviour tracking
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Which quotes you read, save or share

On your device

Your settings (such as the daily reminder time), the Mottos you save and a copy of your progress are stored on your device so the app opens instantly, even offline. A small marker in your device's Keychain remembers that you have already received the welcome gift of feathers; it stays on this device and is not synced.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Motto keeps your progress in your own iCloud account so it follows you across your devices and survives reinstalling the app:

  • The days you have unlocked and the history of your feathers (the welcome gift, packs you bought, and feathers you spent) are stored in your private iCloud database.
  • Your feather balance, saved Mottos and a few preferences are stored in iCloud Key-Value Storage.

This data is managed by Apple, belongs to your Apple ID and is not accessible to us. You can turn off iCloud for Motto, or delete its data, in your device's Settings at any time.

Checking the date

Each day's Motto unlocks on its date. To check today's date, the app makes a simple request to apple.com and reads the date from the reply. Nothing about you is sent with it beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, which Apple receives under its own privacy policy.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device as a local notification. No notification server is involved, and you can turn it off in Motto or in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases

Motto is free. You can buy packs of feathers, which unlock days you missed or days ahead. They are one-off purchases, not a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store your payment details.

To confirm purchases and add the feathers to your balance, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases. When the app loads the feather packs, and when you buy a pack or restore purchases, it sends RevenueCat:

  • A random ID created by the app on your device. It isn't your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and we have no way to connect it to you.
  • The purchase details the App Store provides: which pack was bought, when, the price and currency, and the country of your App Store account.
  • Basic technical details needed to answer the request, such as the app version, the iOS version and the device's language.

This is used only to confirm your purchases and to show us totals such as how many packs were sold. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a Motto or your Motty postcard, the image is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Motto doesn't see where you send it.

Feedback

"Send feedback" opens your own email app with a message to us. We only receive what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and improve Motto.

No account required

Motto does not ask you to create an account or sign in. Your Motto is ready as soon as you open the app.

Third-party services

Besides Apple, the only third-party service in Motto is RevenueCat, used for purchases as described above. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Children

Motto is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at feedbackmotto@gmail.com.
